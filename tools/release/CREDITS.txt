VOICE
-----
The questions and explanations are read by a recorded neural voice, "Andrew"
(en-US-AndrewNeural), generated with Microsoft's Edge text-to-speech service
through the open-source edge-tts tool. The recordings ship inside the app; no
internet connection is used to play them. The other named voices in the voice
list (Ava, Brian, Emma and the rest) are Microsoft Edge neural voices,
fetched from the same service only when picked; they need an internet
connection. Microsoft and
