# 2023 Journeyman Final Exam #1

## Exam Profile

- 70 questions
- 3-hour time limit
- Open-book NEC study practice
- Self-grade: correct answers divided by total questions

## Topics Covered

- Load calculations and demand factors
- Conductor ampacity with temperature and conduit fill adjustments
- Wiring methods, cable protection, and conduit fill
- Grounding electrodes and grounding conductors
- GFCI and AFCI protection
- Receptacle placement and ratings
- Switchboards, switchgear, and equipment marking
- Motors, transformers, welders, and special equipment
- Troubleshooting with voltage and circuit measurements
- Diagrams, symbols, formulas such as W = E x I

## Study Method

Use the verified answer and NEC reference for each question to locate the rule in the code book. Question wording, answer choices, correct answers, and supplied code provisions are kept as separate data.

## Timing

The source exam uses one overall 3-hour clock. That averages about 2 minutes 34 seconds per question for the 70-question set. In the app it runs on the full exam's pace of 3 minutes per question (210 minutes for the 70 questions).
